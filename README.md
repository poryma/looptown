# LOOP TOWN

A 20-stage pixel simulation on how algorithms grow rumors and stereotypes — feedback loops, click loops, defaults, and data recycling.
Made for **PUBS279 AI and Society** (Korea University, Fall 2026), Week 6: Algorithmic Bias.

**Play:** open the GitHub Pages link of this repository. Choose 한국어 · 中文 · English on the first screen.

- Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type or click is sent anywhere; progress is saved only in your own browser.
- Mint and Peach folk are fictional groups, not stand-ins for any real group.
- The simulations are simplified teaching models, not empirical results.
